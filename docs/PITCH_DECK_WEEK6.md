## Storyteller Guidelines & Pitch Delivery Protocol

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Coach Catherine Mapomba's Directive:**
> *"Do not default to the most technical developer as the presenter. Choose your team's most compelling storyteller. Judges will remember the human narrative, the conviction in your voice, and the clarity of your problem-solution bridge far more than code syntax."*

### Delivery Principles for the Presenter:
1. **Pacing:** Speak at an unhurried, measured pace of ~130–140 words per minute. Total spoken script is ~530 words, leaving natural breathing room and dramatic pauses.
2. **Pauses & Cues:** Observe the bracketed cues: `[pause 1s]` for concept digestion, and `[click slide]` for seamless slide transitions.
3. **Eye Contact & Energy:** Look directly into the camera or the judges' eyes. Do not read off the screen—rehearse until the script feels like a personal conversation.
4. **Emotional Arc:** Move from visceral frustration on Slide 2, through intellectual clarity on Slides 3–5, to grounded humility and grit on Slide 8, finishing with unstoppable optimism and urgency on Slide 10.
